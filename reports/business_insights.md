# Comprehensive Business Insights & Executive Report

**Project Title:** End-to-End E-Commerce Sales Analysis & Business Intelligence Dashboard  
**Dataset:** Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset (99,441 orders, 2016–2018)  
**Author:** Data Analyst / BI Analyst  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This study conducted a thorough investigation into 99,441 transactional e-commerce orders, evaluating commercial revenue, category performance, regional dynamics, delivery operations, and customer retention.

### Key Performance Highlights:
- **Total Gross Revenue:** **$15,419,773.75** (comprising **$13,221,498.11** in net product sales and **$2,198,275.64** in freight fees).
- **Total Completed Orders:** **96,478 delivered orders**.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** **$137.04**.
- **Customer Base:** **93,358 unique customers**.
- **Repeat Purchase Rate:** **3.00%** (2,801 repeat customers).

## 3. Customer Retention & Purchasing Behavior

Analysis of customer ordering frequency revealed a clear marketplace dynamic:
- **One-time buyers:** 90,557 customers (**97.00%**)
- **Repeat buyers (2 orders):** 2,573 customers (**2.76%**)
- **Loyal buyers (3+ orders):** 228 customers (**0.24%**)

### Root Cause Analysis:
1. **Marketplace Discovery Platform:** Olist functions primarily as a multi-seller intermediary rather than a single branded store. Customers often purchase specific items without building brand affinity.
2. **Post-Purchase Engagement Deficit:** Absence of cross-category post-purchase remarketing and loyalty programs causes significant customer churn after the first order.

---

## 4. Regional Distribution & Logistics Impact

### Regional Sales Concentration:
- **São Paulo (SP)** is by far the commercial engine of the platform:
  - Generates **$5,067,633.16** (38.3% of total revenue) across **40,501 orders**.
  - Enjoys an average delivery duration of **8.7 days** due to high seller concentration in the Southeast.
- **Rio de Janeiro (RJ)** and **Minas Gerais (MG)** rank 2nd and 3rd ($1.76M and $1.55M), but experience longer transit times (15.1 days and 12.0 days).
- Northern and Northeastern states experience average delivery times exceeding **20–28 days**, which suppresses order frequency and satisfaction.

### Logistics Correlation with Customer Satisfaction:
| Review Score | Total Orders | Average Delivery Days | Delayed Orders (%) |

| ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5 Stars) | 56,810 | **10.7 days** | 3.00% |

> **Critical Takeaway:** Delivery speed and fulfillment reliability are the single biggest determinants of customer reviews. Nearly **38% of all 1-star reviews are directly caused by shipping delays**, with 1-star orders taking an average of **21.3 days** compared to **10.7 days** for 5-star reviews.

## 5. Strategic Recommendations for Business Stakeholders

1. **Regional Fulfillment Hubs (FBA Model):**
   - Establish decentralized warehousing in Rio de Janeiro (RJ) and southern distribution nodes (PR/RS) to reduce transit times from 15+ days to under 8 days.
2. **SLA Penalties & Preferred Carrier Routing:**
   - Partner with regional courier services for long-distance routes rather than standard national postal lines, directly targeting the 37.8% delay rate on 1-star reviews.
3. **Retention & Loyalty Campaigns:**
   - Introduce automated post-purchase vouchers valid for complementary categories (e.g., offering a discount on "Health & Beauty" 14 days after a "Bed, Bath & Table" delivery).
4. **Category Expansion for High-AOV Verticals:**
   - Onboard verified sellers in premium, high-margin categories such as *Watches & Gifts* and *Computers Accessories*, which deliver higher revenue per fulfillment cost.
